Epic Universe Wait Times in Review: September 2026

October 1, 2026

A data-centric breakdown of when wait times peak, which days of the week have the lowest waits, and which rides are a risky bet during EPA, all from 45,361 wait-time readings.

The lowest park median yet

The park median fell to 25 minutes, down from 30 in August and 35 in June and July. The median standby wait starts around 10 minutes in the first hour, jumps to a 40-minute peak from 11 AM–12 PM, then slides steadily to about 15 after 7 PM. Midday runs roughly 2.3x the evening, the biggest late-day discount of the summer.

Early Park Admission was back to a steady 9 AM almost all month. The one exception was Tuesday, September 29, when EPA opened at 8 AM. That day is counted in every number here, but the cover chart shows only September 1–28, since a single day is too thin to chart on its own.

The first hour and the last two hours still have the lowest wait times.

Weekends and Mondays were busier, Tuesday through Friday was the bargain

The week split cleanly in two. Saturday, Sunday, and Monday all ran a 30-minute median, while Tuesday through Friday held at 25. Wednesday had the lowest average of the week (33). The busiest day of the month was Monday, September 14 (median 40) and the calmest was Tuesday, September 1 (median 15).

Attraction uptime

Uptime is the share of time a ride was operating during park hours, with downtime split between weather and everything else. Weather eased off: delays hit on 16 of 30 days, down from 25 in August, and most of the least reliable rides improved. Hiccup’s Wing Gliders was again the least reliable, but at 81% (up from 74%), with Fyre Drill close behind at 82%. Mine-Cart Madness climbed to 84% from 75%. Monsters Unchained bounced back to 97% after its rough end to August.

The ride that went the other way was Battle at the Ministry, down to 85% from 93%. None of its downtime was weather: it lost about 15% of its operating time to other closures, the most of any ride in the park, across 95 closures on 27 days. Hiccup’s Wing Gliders led the closure count with 103, and had at least one closure on every day of the month. Fyre Drill closed less often (43), but its closures averaged 76 minutes, the longest in the park.

The EPA gamble, ranked

Battle at the Ministry became the riskiest EPA bet at 40% of mornings (12 of 30), up from 26% in August. Most of its delays were short (15-minute median), but a few long ones pulled the average up to 42. Mine-Cart Madness was next at 30%, up from 23%. Stardust Racers fell to 23% from 39%, though when it did go down it stayed down longer (35-minute median delay). Hiccup’s Wing Gliders improved the most, from 32% to 13%. Bowser Jr. Challenge and Dragon Racer’s Rally were the safe picks with just one delayed morning each. Curse of the Werewolf, Fyre Drill, Monsters Unchained, and Constellation Carousel did not open for EPA, so they aren’t ranked.

Notes for September 2026

  • The park median fell to 25 minutes, but the two biggest lines got longer: Battle at the Ministry rose to 90 (from 70) and Mine-Cart Madness to 90 (from 85). Mario Kart eased to 45 from 50.
  • Battle at the Ministry logged the single worst wait we tracked: 230 minutes on September 23. It peaks late, 120 from 3–4 PM, and was cheapest at either end of the day (75 in the 9 AM hour and the 7 PM hour). Wednesday was its best day at 65; Friday was its worst at 120.
  • Mine-Cart Madness peaks around 120 at noon. The EPA hour was the cheapest at 60, so it’s still worth the gamble if you can stomach the 30% delay odds.
  • Mario Kart is the clearest EPA win: 15 minutes in the 9 AM hour against a 70-minute peak at 11 AM. It drops back to 15 by the 7 PM hour.
  • Shorter September hours mean the 7 PM hour was usually the last full one. Plan your evening rides for 6–7 PM rather than counting on an 8 PM hour.
  • September 29 was a special-event night: rides closed around 6 PM and reopened for a separately ticketed event until about 10:30 PM. We left that evening out of every number here, so event-night waits don’t skew the regular-day picture and the 6 PM close isn’t counted as a breakdown.
  • Skip Saturday through Monday if you can. Tuesday through Friday ran 5 minutes shorter across the park.
